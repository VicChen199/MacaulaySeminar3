n is the number of neighborhoods in the Pearson test. The primary n is 32.
r is the Pearson correlation between traffic_mean_vehicles_per_hour and asthma_ed_rate_per_10000. r=-0.08080239123990451
p-value is the two-sided tail probability under a null correlation of 0. p=0.6602110540504577
confidence interval is the 95 percent interval for that correlation, from -0.4177267812045329 to 0.27565833701897735.
directional reading is the sign of r. The primary sign is negative.
cross-period consistency is whether the sign and the interval agree across tested periods.
ecological limit is that a neighborhood pattern is not an individual-level pattern.
